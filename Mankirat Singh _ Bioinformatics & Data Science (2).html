Résumé

Research, lab and data work

  1. May 2026 to now

    Master thesis researcher

    IBIMA Plataforma BIONAND, Málaga, Spain

    How do gut microbes and faecal metabolites change after bariatric surgery, and do they change together?

    • Hypothesis: surgery reshapes the gut microbiota and faecal metabolome in a coordinated way that contributes to the metabolic improvements patients see, and Roux-en-Y gastric bypass (RYGB) and sleeve gastrectomy (SG) may drive different adaptations
    • Microbiome: processing raw Ion Torrent 16S rRNA data in QIIME2 into high-quality taxonomic profiles
    • Metabolomics: exploratory analysis of faecal metabolomics to find metabolites linked to surgery
    • Comparisons: before vs after surgery, RYGB vs SG, and patients with obesity vs a healthy lean reference cohort
    • Integration: linking taxa and metabolites in mixOmics to find microbiota–metabolite signatures; 15+ host–microbiota interactions identified so far
    QIIME2RmixOmics16S rRNAMetabolomics
